MKAYFX V4 is an educational market-analysis interface. XAU/USD liquidity levels, heatmaps, pressure, synthetic CVD and footprint allocations are heuristic functions of OHLC or optional provider-reported candle volume. They cannot measure actual resting bids and asks, hidden institutional orders, aggressive trade initiators or real executed volume without a suitable order-book and trade feed. Scores are not calibrated probabilities; no BUY/SELL orders are generated automatically.
